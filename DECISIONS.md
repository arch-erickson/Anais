# Open decisions

These were the wireframe notes on the site (removed from the pages on 2026-11-25). Each is still
a question for the salon owner.

## Salon home
- **Color Studio:** free consult for new color guests? In person, virtual, or both?
- **How it works:** does the salon take walk-ins every day, or only some days? Who sends the
  follow-up message, and how many days after?

## Stylists
- How many stylists? Show levels (Junior / Senior / Director) with different pricing?

## Services
- Confirm the categories and the services inside each one, especially nails (from her Facebook
  manicure / pedicure posts) and brows & lashes.

## Gallery
- Live Instagram feed, or hand-picked photos? (127 salon photos from Facebook are in
  photos/facebook.)

## Barbershop
- Barbershop name. Walk-ins welcome, or appointment only?
- Monthly membership (e.g. two cuts a month)?

## Booking / visit
- Each stylist's and barber's real WhatsApp number (now placeholders +1 555 010 00xx), and the
  front-desk numbers used for "No preference".
- Real salon hours for the hours list (the booking itself is open every day; each stylist
  confirms their own schedule).
- Deposit or cancellation policy?
