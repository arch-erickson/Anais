The Color Studio

Color is our craft.

Every color service starts with a consultation. We read your skin tone, your history and how you live with your hair, then build color that grows out beautifully.

  • Balayage & lived-in color
  • Blonding & foil highlights
  • Vivid & fashion color
  • Rich brunettes & reds
  • Grey blending & coverage
  • Color correction

Your color visit

Three steps to your color.

  1. 1

    Consult

    We talk goals, history and upkeep. Patch and strand tests when needed.

  2. 2

    Color

    Your colorist builds the formula and places it by hand, then we tone and gloss.

  3. 3

    Care

    You leave with a home-care plan and your next visit on the books.
