# Flower art credits

The gold flowers and leaves in the intro animation come from two free sets on SVG Repo.
Both use a **Creative Commons Attribution** license: you may use them commercially,
but the site must credit the artists somewhere visible (the footer is fine).

| Files | Set | Artist | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `leaf-338…`, `grass-338073…` | Botanical Hand Drawn Vectors | stocklution | https://www.svgrepo.com/collection/botanical-hand-drawn-vectors/ |
| `bloom-427…` | Abstract Flower Handdrawn Vectors | twiri (dribbble.com/twiri) | https://www.svgrepo.com/collection/abstract-flower-handdrawn-vectors/ |

Suggested footer line:

> Botanical illustrations by stocklution and twiri via SVG Repo (CC BY).

The files here are cleaned copies of the originals: comments and doctype removed, color
set to `currentColor` so they take on whatever color you give them in CSS. The intro uses
the same drawings from `js/intro-flowers.js`.
